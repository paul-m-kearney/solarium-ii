The President's Decision

Project Solarium II, Round 1: December 2026 to November 2030

Statement

America will finish its war in the Middle East and turn to the Pacific. We will seek a deal with Iran that ends the blockade, opens Hormuz, and lets inspectors in. Israel, Saudi Arabia, and the UAE will lead their region's defense; anyone who kills Americans will answer for it. We will rebuild our industry with the urgency of the years after Sputnik. Europe will lead its own defense and pay for Ukraine's, with America first among equals and a ready arsenal. If China blockades Taiwan, we and our partners will impose sanctions, then broaden them and escort shipping if it persists.

Red line (Task Force A)

A blockade of Taiwan by any Chinese state-directed actor, including turning around commercial vessels, mining port approaches, or closing airspace, violates freedom of navigation and endangers peace under the Taiwan Relations Act.

If crossed, America will: Impose targeted sanctions, together with Japan, Europe, and other partners, on the Chinese officials and companies involved in a blockade of Taiwan, to be lifted when commercial shipping is restored. Broaden the sanctions and join allied naval escorts of commercial shipping if China does not comply.
