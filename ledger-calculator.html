Check before you submit

    The rules, from the Player Handbook (section 5): each vector moves its indicator by 0.8 × (points − 15) over the round, so every balanced ledger nets +8. Three-quarters of an Industry gain lands within the round; the rest arrives in the two years after. A theater under 10 points heats up (+2 tension) unless an adopted Signature Initiative in that theater hands it to named allies. A theater with 30 or more points calms down (−2 tension) only if Military and Diplomacy together have at least 30. The President may blend the three ledgers, which pulls every number toward the middle. The world's response, not this formula, decides how strong America ends up.
